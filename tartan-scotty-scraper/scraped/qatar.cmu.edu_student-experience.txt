Life at CMU-Q
Your life as a CMU-Q student is about exploring new interests, building a community of friends and faculty,  and figuring out where your career path will lead you. This is where you belong.
CMU-Q’s Student Affairs team is here to support and foster your intellectual and personal growth, and to help you explore and experience university life. We care about you, your studies, your social growth, your well-being, and your future.
Get Involved
When classes are over, there is still so much to do at CMU-Q. By exploring leadership development, joining student clubs, or contributing to civic and community engagement projects, you will build your leadership skills and expand your horizons.
Get Involved at CMU-Q
Prepare for Your Career
Throughout the year, CMU-Q students have many opportunities to develop their skills, build their resumes, and prepare for their careers after graduation.
Prepare for Your Career at CMU-Q
See the World
Students have many opportunities to learn about the world outside of Qatar, and most will travel for learning experiences during their undergraduate education.
See the World as a CMU-Q Student
Be Well and Thrive
CMU-Q sees each student holistically. During the undergraduate journey, we provide opportunities and support for students to grow in intellectual and artistic curiosity, personal well being, professional development, leadership, social responsibility, and intercultural learning.
