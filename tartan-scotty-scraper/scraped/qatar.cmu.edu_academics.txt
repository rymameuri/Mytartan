Academics
Carnegie Mellon is the kind of university where you can follow your interests, explore your passions, and create a strong foundation for your unique career path. The Qatar campus offers five undergraduate degree programs from the Carnegie Mellon main campus in Pittsburgh, USA. Our graduates earn Carnegie Mellon degrees with identical requirements as their classmates in Pittsburgh.
Degree Programs
Artificial Intelligence
Biological Sciences
Business Administration
Computer Science
Information Systems
Foundation in the Humanities and Sciences
All degree programs are built on a foundation in the sciences and humanities. Students deepen their understanding of English and mathematics, and then follow their special interests in areas like Arabic studies, history, physics, psychology, and languages.
