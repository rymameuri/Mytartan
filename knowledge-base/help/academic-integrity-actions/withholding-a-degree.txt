Withholding a Degree
A degree from the university signifies not only successful completion of academic requirements but also compliance with the university’s standards and good standing as a member of the university community. The dean of students or designee may direct that a degree not be certified by the university registrar and therefore not awarded pending the resolution of academic integrity actions proceedings, including completion of all outcomes.
