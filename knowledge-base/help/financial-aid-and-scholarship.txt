Financial Aid

Applications for financial assistance should be directed to Qatar Foundation (QF). For more information, see http://www.myeducationcity.com/

Students can contact QF's Financial Aid Office (FAO) directly by calling +974 4454 0400 or by emailing fao@qf.org.qa.

Financial Aid for U.S. Citizens

Please note that U.S. citizens, and some non-citizens (ex. green card holders) may be eligible for Federal Financial Aid (FAFSA) to support your studies at Carnegie Mellon University in Qatar. There are different types of funding within FAFSA, including grants (no repayment) and loans.

We advise all U.S. citizens to apply for FAFSA. For guidance on how to apply, see: http://www.cmu.edu/finaid/

If you are not a U.S. citizen, but think you might be eligible, please check: http://studentaid.ed.gov/eligibility

If you are applying for Financial Aid from Qatar Foundation, please understand that you can receive FAFSA funding in addition. The university does provide QF with information regarding students' FAFSA awards to avoid students receiving more than 100% funding.

Both QF funding and FAFSA will be posted directly to your student account. The amount awarded per year will be posted in 2 installments: one half for fall and one half for spring.

For questions about financial aid, please contact Sheila Rian – Enrollment Services Manager.

Need-Based Grant

CMU-Q provides a world-class financial aid program to its admitted students. Beginning with students who entered in August 2019, the QF need-based grant program provides grant aid to students of all nationalities through the Qatar Foundation. Grants (aid with no repayment) of up to the full cost of attendance are made to families based on their unique financial circumstances.

The QF need-based grant program provides an outstanding opportunity for all students to receive funding for their studies at CMU-Q.

QF Need-Based Grant Guidelines — available on Scotty.

Scholarship

Scholarship Application Requirements and deadlines for QF scholarships application for academic year 2026-2027.

TAFAWQ – تفّق Merit Scholarship

Coverage:
- Tuition and books
- Housing for non-resident students and residents living outside Doha
- Summer courses and Study abroad
- Monthly stipends

Requirements:
- Required cumulative GPA 4.0
- Complete two consecutive semesters at an Education City undergraduate program
- Submit TAFAWQ application on FAS
- Include a passport copy, national ID, and QID
- Undergo a Criminal Evidence and Information Department (CEID) clearance (for students who are residents of Qatar)
- Submit your updated official transcript
- Provide two recommendation letters
- Share your community service, leadership, and intellectual contribution profile
- Take the Arabic language placement test and complete any required classes based on the evaluation
- Attend an interview as part of the selection process
- Payment of application fees QAR 183 (50 USD) upon application submission

TAMAIYUZ Scholarship

Coverage:
- Tuition and books
- Housing for non-resident students and residents living outside Doha
- Summer courses and Study abroad

Requirements:
- Required cumulative GPA 3.8 or higher
- Complete two consecutive semesters at an Education City undergraduate program
- Submit a financial need assessment online (application name TAMAIYUZ on FAS)
- Include a passport copy, national ID, and QID
- Undergo a Criminal Evidence and Information Department (CEID) clearance (for students who are residents of Qatar)
- Submit your official transcript
- Provide two recommendation letters
- Take the Arabic language placement test and complete any required classes based on the evaluation
- Payment of application fees QAR 183 (50 USD) upon application submission
- Cycle will be open on 15 May 2026 until 5 June 2026

Start your application from: https://fas.qf.org.qa

For any concerns, technical issues, or inquiries – email sch@qf.org.qa and the scholarship team will respond within 3 working days (excluding holidays).
