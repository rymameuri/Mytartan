Publications
Publications produced independently by students must clearly state that they do not carry the endorsement of Carnegie Mellon University. All university-sanctioned student publications must be approved by the Office of the Dean of Students, and they should include the official
CMU wordmark
and
Statement of Assurance
. Visit the
brand guidelines
for more information.
