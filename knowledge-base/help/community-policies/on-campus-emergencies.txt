On-Campus Emergencies
Students are expected to immediately contact University Police at 412-268-2323 when police, fire, or medical personnel are needed for an on-campus emergency situation. Off-campus emergencies should be directed to 911.
The Environmental Health and Safety department provides additional information about emergency
procedures via the University Safety Guidelines:
https://www.cmu.edu/ehs/Guidelines/index.html
