              Broadcasting
Broadcasting
All students who participate in commercial, off-campus television or radio broadcasts, webcasts or podcasts and use the Carnegie Mellon name should consult with the Media Relations Department. These students should contact Media Relations at 412-268-2900 prior to their participation.
