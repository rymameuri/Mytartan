Choosing a minor at CMU-Q is a planning decision, not a personality test.

From the official minors page:
- Declare by the end of sophomore year if you can, so you have time to finish the courses.
- A declared minor may get priority enrollment in required courses. Declaration does not guarantee a seat.
- Submit fillable PDF forms (no handwriting) to qatareducationform@andrew.cmu.edu at least two weeks before registration week.
- To drop or change a minor, email Jarrin Nevel so your degree audit updates.
- Track requirements yourself in the Academic Audit / Stellic.

Minors listed on Scotty include Arabic Studies, Biological Sciences, Business Administration, Business Analytics and Optimization, Computational Biology, Computer Science, Economics, Financial Management, History, Mathematical Sciences, Neuroscience, Professional Writing, Psychology, Sociology, and Tech Entrepreneurship. Student-defined minors need Associate Vice Provost approval and the same forms inbox.

This page does not invent which minor is "best" for a major. Ask your area advisor for program fit, and use Stellic for the course list.
