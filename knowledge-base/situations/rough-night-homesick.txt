A rough night or homesick stretch is common at CMU-Q, especially away from home in Education City. You do not have to perform being fine.

What is actually here:
- Student Affairs exists to support your well-being and campus life, not only your transcript.
- If you want a real counselor, CaPS-Q offers free, confidential counseling and walk-in support for urgent needs. Contact Dr. Atorina Benjamin at atorinab@andrew.cmu.edu. Scotty is not a therapist and will not diagnose you.
- International and exchange students can reach Meredith Lu (meredit2@andrew.cmu.edu) for onboarding, housing, and settling-in questions already listed on the New International and Exchange Students page.
- If you just want people, Student Activities (Nagla Abdelhady, nabdelha@andrew.cmu.edu) and Student Engagement (Juan Sam, juans@andrew.cmu.edu) are the club and involvement contacts on the Student Affairs pages.

You can stay in this chat. That counts. If you want a next step, pick one: sit with Scotty for a bit, book a quiet library room, or email CaPS-Q when you are ready.

No invented crisis hotlines or phone numbers. Those are not in the CMU-Q knowledge base, so they are omitted on purpose.
