Midterms week at CMU-Q is a lot. You do not have to invent a new system tonight.

What actually exists on this campus (from official pages, not guesswork):
- Supplemental Instruction through the Academic Resource Center is free, peer-led, and starts in weeks 1-2 of class. It is not meant as a last-minute cram substitute for class. Ask your SI leader or email Mariamma Thomas (mariamma@andrew.cmu.edu) or Afthab Azeez (afthaba@andrew.cmu.edu).
- The CMU-Q Library has bookable study rooms 1153 and 1155 (Google Calendar) if you want a quiet block. Reserve textbooks are in-library, up to 3 hours.
- If the stress stops being "I have three exams" and starts being something you cannot carry, CaPS-Q is free and confidential. Dr. Atorina Benjamin is at atorinab@andrew.cmu.edu. Scotty is a classmate, not a counselor.

A workable next step: pick the exam that is first, do one 45-minute block, then stop. First-year and sophomore students can also write Damian Dourado (ddourado@andrew.cmu.edu) about academic success support.

Do not invent office hours, exam policies, or phone numbers. If a rule is not on an official page, ask Enrollment Services or your area advisor.
