Quiet study and social study are both available at CMU-Q. Pick the one that matches the work, not the one that sounds more serious.

Quiet:
- CMU-Q Library study rooms 1153 and 1155 book via Google Calendar.
- Reserve textbooks stay in the library, up to 3 hours.
- Headphones are in-library use only. The Poetry Garden / room 1158 open area is the courtyard booking if you want air without a full classroom.

Social / together:
- Supplemental Instruction is collaborative on purpose. SI leaders walk groups through hard concepts. Free, not a last-minute dump the night before the exam.
- The Academic Resource Center also points to a Self-Help Study Corner. Ask Mariamma Thomas (mariamma@andrew.cmu.edu) or Afthab Azeez (afthaba@andrew.cmu.edu) what is running this term.

If you just want company while you work, stay here and do a 25-minute sprint. Library hours and SI times that are not on those official pages are not invented here.
