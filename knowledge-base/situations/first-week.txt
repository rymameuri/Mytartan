First week at CMU-Q feels like too many tabs. You do not need the whole year figured out by Friday.

Grounded next steps from official pages:
- Course planning lives in Stellic (academic audit), the Schedule of Classes, and SIO. Registration and schedule-change forms go to Jarrin Nevel at jfn@andrew.cmu.edu.
- First-year and early academic success support: Damian Dourado, Director of Student Academic Success, ddourado@andrew.cmu.edu.
- New international and exchange students are pointed to the New Student Website, Qatar ID registration, a local bank account, and Education City tram / Doha metro maps. Contact Meredith Lu at meredit2@andrew.cmu.edu.
- Supplemental Instruction sessions start in weeks 1-2. That is the moment to find your SI leader, not week 8.

Do not invent orientation times, building hours, or your assigned faculty advisor. Named first-year advisors live in SIO, not in this knowledge base.

If you are lost, start with one question: "What do I need to register?" or "Who do I email if Stellic looks wrong?" Then we go one step at a time.
