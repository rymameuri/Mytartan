Alpha Iota Delta Award — CMU-Q

The Alpha Iota Delta Award is a recognition given to outstanding students in the Information Systems / Decision Sciences field.

Alpha Iota Delta is the international honor society for students in the decision sciences and information systems disciplines.

CMU-Q administers this award to recognize excellence in this academic area.

Awardee lists and eligibility criteria are provided on the myCMU-Q portal page.
