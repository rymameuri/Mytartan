Washington Semester Program — Education Office, CMU-Q

Place undergraduates in DC's policy ecosystem — embassies, think tanks, NGOs.

Duration: One semester.

Activities: ~24 hours/week internship + CMU classes 3-4 afternoons/evenings per week.

Application Checklist — Submit to Emily Half (ehalf@andrew.cmu.edu):
- Statement of purpose
- Resume
- Transcript
- 2 faculty recommendation letters
- Academic plan
- Internship preferences

Deadlines: Listed by semester on portal page.

Visa: J-1 student visa required; OIE Pittsburgh assists.

Health Insurance: CMU Student Health Insurance available.

Minor: Can earn Politics and Public Policy minor by completing required coursework (73-102 + others).
