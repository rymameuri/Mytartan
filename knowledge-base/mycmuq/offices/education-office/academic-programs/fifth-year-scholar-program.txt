Fifth-Year Scholar Program — Education Office, CMU-Q

Established 1991 through student-faculty collaboration.

Provides tuition-free fellowship for a full fifth year of study.

Enables deeper exploration, minors, research, or extracurricular involvement.

Eligibility: Typically third-year students on track to complete degree in 4 years.

Requirements: Strong academic standing, faculty endorsement, compelling project proposal.
