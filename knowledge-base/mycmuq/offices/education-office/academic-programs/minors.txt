Minors — Education Office, CMU-Q

16 Available Minors — declare by end of sophomore year.

Humanities:
- Arabic Studies — Language proficiency and Arabic cultural understanding. Advisor: Ezzohra Moufid.
- History — Writing, argumentation, source analysis. Advisor: Benjamin Reilly. Prereq: 79-104.
- Professional Writing — Writing/communications career preparation. Advisor: Tom Mitchell.

Business & Economics:
- Business Administration — Functional areas of business; application required. Advisor: Agustin Indaco.
- Business Analytics & Optimization — Data-driven business decisions. Advisor: Fuad Farooqi. Prereq: Multivariate Calculus.
- Economics — Economic theory and data analysis. Advisor: Agustin Indaco.
- Financial Management — Financial markets; mirrors Finance concentration. Advisor: Fuad Farooqi.

Science & Technology:
- Biological Sciences — Biochemistry, genetics, cell biology. Advisor: Mohamed Bouaouina.
- Computational Biology — Mathematical/computational methods in biology. Advisor: Ryan Riley.
- Computer Science — Algorithms, data structures, programming paradigms. Advisor: Ryan Riley. Prereq: 21-127, 15-112.
- Mathematical Sciences — Real analysis, discrete math, linear algebra. Advisor: Zelealem Yilma. Prereq: 21-127.
- Tech Entrepreneurship — Build tech-driven startups from ideation to investor pitch. Advisor: Nui Vatanasakdakul.

Social Sciences:
- Neuroscience — Natural, social, and computer sciences curriculum. Advisor: Nesrine Affara.
- Psychology — 8-course curriculum covering psychological theories and scientific method. Advisor: Jennifer Bruder.
- Sociology — Social networks, technology, empirical inquiry. Advisor: Jocelyn Belanger.

Student-Defined Minor (SDM): Custom academic path; contact Education Office.
