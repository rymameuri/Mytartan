Supplemental Instruction — Academic Resource Center, CMU-Q

Peer-led academic assistance program.

SI Leaders are students who previously excelled in the course.

Sessions: guide students through difficult concepts using collaborative study techniques.

Free and available to all students; sessions start weeks 1-2 of class.

Not a substitute for attending class; not a last-minute strategy.

SI Leader announced on first day of class; also posted online.
