Phi Alpha Theta Honor Society — Education Office, CMU-Q

Phi Alpha Theta is the international honor society for History.

Membership requirements:
- Completed minimum 4 courses in History
- Minimum GPA of 3.1 in History
- Overall GPA of 3.0 or better
