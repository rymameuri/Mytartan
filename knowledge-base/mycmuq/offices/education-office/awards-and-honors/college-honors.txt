College Honors — Education Office, CMU-Q

College honors are conferred to students who complete a senior honors thesis. Admission criteria vary by major. College honors appear on transcripts.

By college/cord color:

Dietrich College (purple cord):
Information Systems students are awarded Dietrich Humanities & Social Sciences Senior Honors if they maintain a QPA of 3.25 or higher overall, a QPA of 3.5 within their major, complete a thesis, and present it at Meeting of the Minds.

Mellon College of Science (yellow cord):
Biological Sciences students are awarded MCS Research Honors if they fulfill the college's research thesis and GPA requirements.

School of Computer Science (tiffany gold cord):
Computer Science students who complete a senior honors thesis under SCS criteria.

Tepper School of Business (drab brown cord):
Business Administration students meeting Tepper's honors thesis requirements.
