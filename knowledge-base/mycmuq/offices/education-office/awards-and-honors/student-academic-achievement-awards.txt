Student Academic Achievement Awards — Education Office, CMU-Q

Overview of all academic awards and honors at CMU-Q:

- College Honors — conferred to senior completing honors thesis; criteria based on academic excellence and faculty approval
- University Honors — cumulative QPA ≥ 3.5 after 7 semesters of full-time enrollment
- Qatar Campus Scholar Award — 4 students per senior class; represent best integration of academic and personal achievements. Previous awardees listed 2016-2024
- Andrew Carnegie Scholar (ACS) — 1 annual award to a senior; high academic excellence + multidimensional achievement. Previous awardees 2016-2023
- Outstanding Academic Achievement Award — highest GPA per major at graduation. Previous awardees 2015-2022
- Outstanding Course Assistant Award — exemplary CA contributions. Previous awardees 2019-2026
- Phi Alpha Theta Honor Society — history honor society; requires minimum 4 history courses. Previous awardees 2018-2022

See individual award sub-pages for detailed criteria and awardee lists.
