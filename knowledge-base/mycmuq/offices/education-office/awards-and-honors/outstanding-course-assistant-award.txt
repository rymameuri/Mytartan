Outstanding Course Assistant Award — Education Office, CMU-Q

Recognizes exemplary contributions of student tutors/CAs. Administered by the Academic Resource Center on behalf of the Associate Dean for Education.

Eligibility: Current CMU-Q students serving in a Course Assistant role.

How to Nominate: Faculty or students may submit nominations before the published deadline.

Selection Criteria:
- Demonstrates knowledge of field and commitment to student success
- Shows interest in subject, is engaging and enthusiastic
- Explains course content clearly and logically
- Goes above and beyond in scheduling flexibility and instructional support
- Facilitates a supportive learning environment
- Provides support enabling students to discover answers on their own
- Models excellent problem-solving strategies
- Encourages student participation and motivation
- Provides timely, constructive feedback
- Respects students' diverse backgrounds and values
- Shows evidence of working collaboratively

Tips for a Strong Nomination:
- Students: Describe one or two specific tutoring sessions and articulate the CA's particular strengths
- Faculty: Focus on specific examples of personal attitude and professional skills; elaborate on observations demonstrating leadership qualities
