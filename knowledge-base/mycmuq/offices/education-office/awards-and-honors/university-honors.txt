University Honors — Education Office, CMU-Q

Students maintaining a cumulative QPA of at least 3.5 after seven semesters of full-time enrollment are awarded University Honors.

Awardee lists are maintained going back to 2015 (2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and subsequent years).

Each year includes dozens of recipients drawn from all four majors (Biological Sciences, Business Administration, Computer Science, Information Systems).
