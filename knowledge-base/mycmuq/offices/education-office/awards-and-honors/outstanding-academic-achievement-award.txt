Outstanding Academic Achievement Award — Education Office, CMU-Q

One student per major (BA, BS, CS, IS) is selected based on highest GPA at time of graduation.

Candidates include Spring graduates of the calendar year plus Summer/Fall graduates of the previous year.

Past Awardees:
2022: Laila Assami (Biological Sciences), Enas Mohamad Mohsen (Business Administration), Dorde Popovic (Computer Science), Fabiha Tahsin (Information Systems)
2021: Noora Ibrahim S A Al-Mohannadi (Biological Sciences), and one per other major
2020, 2019, 2018, 2017, 2016, 2015: 4–5 awardees each year going back to program inception

Full awardee lists by year available on the myCMU-Q portal.
