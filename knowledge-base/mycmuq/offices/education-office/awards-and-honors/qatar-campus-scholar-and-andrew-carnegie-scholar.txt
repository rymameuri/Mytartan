Qatar Campus Scholar and Andrew Carnegie Scholar — Education Office, CMU-Q

Qatar Campus Scholar:
Four students are chosen from the senior class as Qatar Campus Scholars. They represent the best intersection of academic excellence, contributions to the community, volunteerism, and student leadership. Recipients receive a stole to wear with their regalia.

Selection Criteria:
- GPA 3.75 or above
- Must "embody high standards of academic excellence combined with multi-dimensional characteristics"
- No Academic Integrity Violations (exceptions: minor first-year violations)
- Students are invited to apply

Past Awardees: 2016–2024 (4 recipients per year).

Andrew Carnegie Scholar (ACS):
One senior per year. The ACS embodies academic excellence combined with volunteerism, student organization involvement, sports/arts participation, and strong leadership. Chosen from among the four Qatar Campus Scholars. Receives a stole.
