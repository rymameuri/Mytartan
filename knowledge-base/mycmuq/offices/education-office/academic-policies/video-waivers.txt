Video Waivers — Education Office, CMU-Q

Procedure:
1. Complete form
2. Email to Prof. Nesrine Affara
3. Committee review
4. Outcome: approved, denied, or partial approval

FAQ:

No webcam?
Contact helpcenter@qatar.cmu.edu or purchase external webcam.

Privacy concerns about background?
Use Zoom virtual background.

Worried about unauthorized recordings?
Policy strictly prohibits student recording; disability accommodations via Office of Disability Resources.

Contact: Prof. Nesrine Affara
