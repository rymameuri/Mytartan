Tuition and Fees — Education Office, CMU-Q

Tuition: Same as Pittsburgh campus. More info: cmu.edu/sfs/tuition/index.html.

Payment Deadlines: Fall semester and spring semester deadlines (specific dates listed on portal page).

Payment Options: Wire transfer and card payment. See cmu.edu/sfs/billing/payments/qatar/index.html.

E-Bills: Produced last day of each month via Student Information Online (SIO).

Billing Authorizations: Students can authorize e-Bill copies to parents/others via SIO.

Payment Plan: Monthly installment plan available through SIO.

Refunds: Negative balance refunds sent via direct deposit (2-3 business days processing).

Financial Grant Refund: After grant posts, refund processed after 10th day of classes. First-year fall: after 10th day and once Qatar bank account opened.
