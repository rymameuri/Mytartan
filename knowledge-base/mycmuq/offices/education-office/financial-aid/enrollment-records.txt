Enrollment Records — Education Office, CMU-Q

Enrollment Statuses: Full-time, part-time, leave of absence, withdrawn, graduated, and others.

Registration Holds: Can block enrollment even if academically/financially eligible. Types: Financial holds, administrative holds.

Enrollment & Degree Verifications: Order via SIO (Student Information Online). Download PDF, fax, or mail. Visit cmu.edu/hub/records/verifications.

Transcript Request:
- Fill out Transcript Request Form (available at Admissions counter or online)
- Submit to enrollment@qatar.cmu.edu
- Electronic delivery: $8/transcript
- Order via SIO → Academic Records → "Request a Transcript"

My Plaid Student (MPS): Share enrollment verification with family/friends via MPS portal.
