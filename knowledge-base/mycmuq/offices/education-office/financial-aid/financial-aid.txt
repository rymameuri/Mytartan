Financial Aid — Education Office, CMU-Q

Need-Blind Admission: Students admitted without consideration of ability to pay.

Three Sources of Aid:

1. QF Need-Based Grant
Grant aid (no repayment) for students entering Aug 2019+. Apply via fas.qf.org.qa.

2. QF Scholarships (TAFAWQ & TAMAIYUZ):
- TAFAWQ (merit): Min GPA 4.0, monthly stipend, covers tuition/books/housing/study abroad. Criteria: leadership, intellectual contribution, faculty recommendation.
- TAMAIYUZ (need+merit): Min GPA 3.8, need test via FAS. Covers tuition/books/housing.
Application cycle: 15 May – 5 June 2025. Submit via fas.qf.org.qa.

3. FAFSA — for U.S. citizens/eligible non-citizens; stacks on top of QF aid.
