Community Excellence — About CE

The CE Office, committees, and partnerships work toward building a campus culture where respect, inclusion, fairness, and collaboration thrive.

Team:
- Kira Dreher PhD — Associate Dean, Community Excellence / Associate Teaching Professor, English
- Marie France Pantino — Executive Assistant
- Théthé Gwiza Ngalula — Coordinator, Community Engagement and Development

Committees:
- CCCE (Culture, Community & Engagement): Builds social fabric; organizes collaborative activities
- CIE (Institutional Excellence): Considers community and inclusivity from an institutional perspective
- Research & Data Group: Uses quantitative/qualitative methods to explore campus experiences
- Student Advocates: Student-driven; builds student communities and belonging
- ICCC (Improving Campus Climate Committee): First stop for sharing inclusivity concerns
