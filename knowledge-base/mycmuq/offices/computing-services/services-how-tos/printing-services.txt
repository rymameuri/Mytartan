Printing Services — Computing Services Qatar

Overview: Campus Printing via Multi-Function Printers (MFPs); managed by PaperCut MF.

- Faculty/staff can print to any MFP campus-wide
- Students can print to publicly available MFPs
- Available from Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android

Features: Secure release printing (CMU ID card), print-from-anywhere via PaperCut portal (print.qatar.cmu.edu), duplex/color options, scan & copy.

Printer Locations:
Faculty/staff: every pantry and department suite.
Students:
- Hallway behind male prayer room (1st floor)
- Hallway behind female prayer room (2nd floor)
- Library (inside and outside)
- Academic Resource Center (ARC)

A0 Printing (Large Format): Submit print-ready PDF to Computing Services Help Center for poster printing.

Security: Jobs stored securely until released; jobs not released within 8 hours auto-deleted.

Please Print Responsibly: Use duplex and black-and-white; cancel mistaken jobs via PaperCut portal.
