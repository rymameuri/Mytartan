VPN Setup Guide — Computing Services Qatar

Windows, macOS, Linux:
Download Cisco AnyConnect from CMU software center; connect to vpn.qatar.cmu.edu (Qatar) or vpn.cmu.edu (Pittsburgh).

iOS & Android:
Install Cisco AnyConnect app; enter same VPN addresses.

VPN Addresses:
- vpn.qatar.cmu.edu — CMU-Q campus VPN (two access levels)
- vpn.cmu.edu — Pittsburgh campus VPN (two access levels)
