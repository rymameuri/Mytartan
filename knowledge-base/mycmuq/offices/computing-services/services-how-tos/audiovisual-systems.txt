Audiovisual Systems — Computing Services Qatar

Classroom AV Systems:
Video inputs: Podium PC, laptop (USB-C and HDMI), document camera, wireless projection (AirPlay/Miracast).
Video outputs: Projectors/flat-panel displays.
Audio inputs: Program audio from devices, audience microphones, lavalier microphone.
Audio outputs: Wall-mounted and ceiling speakers.
Classroom System Orientation sessions available; interactive guide covers power-on, input switching, USB-C camera/mic access, document camera, lighting/shades, dual-display management, AV Help button.

Conference Room AV Systems:
Single display (projector or large TV), resident PC, HDMI/USB-C laptop inputs, AirPlay/Miracast wireless.
Many rooms feature Zoom Room capability for single-touch meeting join.

Open Areas:
East/West walkways, Food Court, and ground floor assembly area equipped with microphones/speakers for public-address announcements.
Event AV support: submit Event Support Request Form via forms.qatar.cmu.edu.

Events Support:
Computing Services provides technical support for approved events including equipment installation.
Submit Events Support Request Form for customized setup.
