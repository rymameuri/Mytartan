Accounts & Identity — Computing Services Qatar

Eligibility: Students, faculty, staff, alumni, and sponsored guests while they have an active affiliation.

Your Online Identity at CMU:
- Andrew ID (username)
- @andrew.cmu.edu email address
- Preferred name management options

Your Access to CMU Services:
- Andrew Account — gateway to CMU computing
- Password Management — change or reset Andrew password
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) — protects account with a second code or mobile click
