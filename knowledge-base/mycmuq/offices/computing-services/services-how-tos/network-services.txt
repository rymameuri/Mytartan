Network Services — Computing Services Qatar

Wired Network: High-speed up to 1 Gbps; CMU-provided computers only.

Wi-Fi:
- CMUQ-SECURE — for CMU students/faculty/staff with Andrew ID (preferred)
- CMUQ-GUEST — for visitors without Andrew ID or .edu email; SMS-based authentication
- eduroam — for affiliates of participating institutions; use AndrewID@andrew.cmu.edu credentials

VPN:
- vpn.qatar.cmu.edu — CMU-Q campus VPN (two access levels)
- vpn.cmu.edu — Pittsburgh campus VPN (two access levels)

Related sub-pages: Connect to WiFi, VPN Setup Guide
