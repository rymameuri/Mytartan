Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) — Computing Services Qatar

Register for 2FA:
1. Download the DUO Mobile app
2. Follow registration steps on the Two-Factor Authentication Self-Service Tool

Update Your 2FA Device:
Update if you get a new phone or change your number. Use the DUO Self-Service Tool or Instant Restore.

More information: cmu.edu/computing/services/security/identity-access/authentication/how-to/index.html
