Canvas for Students — Computing Services Qatar

Log into Canvas: Navigate to Canvas URL, enter Andrew ID credentials.

Navigate Dashboard: Find enrolled courses; courses appear up to 4 hours after enrollment.

Set Your Time Zone: Account > Settings > Time Zone (set to Qatar/Doha).

View Syllabus & Modules: Click course → Modules or Syllabus tab.

Submit Assignments/Quizzes: Click Assignments, select assignment, upload file or type response.

Check Grades & Feedback: Click Grades in course sidebar.

Announcements & Inbox: Check Announcements tab; use Inbox for messaging instructors.

Mobile Access: Download Canvas Student app (iOS/Android).

Tips: Check Canvas daily, set up notifications, use the calendar.

For Canvas support: helpcenter@qatar.cmu.edu
