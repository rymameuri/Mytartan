AI Hub of Computing Services Qatar — Guide for responsibly using AI tools and resources.

CMU-Approved AI Tools:
- ChatGPT Edu — conversational AI for brainstorming, drafting, research; available to all CMU students, faculty, and staff
- Microsoft Copilot Chat — built into Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint); summarizes documents and automates tasks
- Gemini Web App — Google's AI integrated with CMU Google Workspace; assists with writing and research
- NotebookLM — Google AI for research/study; upload materials for AI-powered summaries
- Zoom AI Companion — generates meeting summaries, highlights action items
- CMU-Q AI Gateway (In Progress) — secure access to CMU-Q's advanced computing resources for faculty and researchers
