Business Systems — Computing Services Qatar

InfoSilem — scheduling/timetabling system; used by Academic Affairs for course and exam scheduling.

Titanium — HIPAA-compliant clinical/appointment management; used by CaPS-Q for counseling appointments.

Odoo — ERP/workflow management (finance, purchasing, inventory, project management); used by Office of Strategic Engagement and Student Affairs.

Asana — cloud-based project/task management; used by Computing Services and administrative units.
