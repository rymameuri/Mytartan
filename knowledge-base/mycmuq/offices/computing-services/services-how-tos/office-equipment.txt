Office Equipment — Computing Services Qatar

Computers:
- Faculty: choose Windows laptop (Lenovo) or MacBook Pro (14" or 16" with Apple Silicon)
- Staff: Windows laptop (Lenovo)

Monitors: Up to two 27-inch 4K displays for all faculty/staff.

Docking Stations: Thunderbolt 3/4, USB4 connectivity; supported models: Lenovo, CalDigit, CableMatters.

Peripherals: Keyboard and mouse standard; headset and webcam available on request.

Office Phones: Cisco IP telephone in every faculty/staff office.

Office Printers: Dedicated office printer by request (justification required); faculty/staff can use campus MFPs with CMU ID card; students use public MFPs.
