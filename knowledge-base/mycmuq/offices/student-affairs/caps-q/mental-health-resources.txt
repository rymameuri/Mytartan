Mental Health Resources — CaPS-Q, CMU-Q

The portal lists a variety of mental health and wellness resources available to students including:
- Headspace (meditation app)
- MindWell-U
- CMU Health & Wellness resources
- Qatar Foundation wellness services
- Mada Accessibility Center
- Local Doha mental health providers

Students in crisis can access 24/7 support through international crisis hotlines and the campus emergency line.
