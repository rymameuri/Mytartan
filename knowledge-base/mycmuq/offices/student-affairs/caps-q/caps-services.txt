CaPS-Q Services — Counseling & Psychological Services Qatar

CaPS-Q provides confidential mental health support to all CMU-Q students.

Services include:
- Individual therapy/counseling
- Crisis intervention
- Consultation
- Outreach and psychoeducation
- Referrals to community mental health resources

All services are free and confidential.

Students can book appointments via the online portal. Walk-in sessions are available for urgent needs.

The CaPS-Q office is located within the Education City campus.

Contact: Dr. Atorina Benjamin — Director, Counseling and Psychological Services — atorinab@andrew.cmu.edu
