Accommodations for Mental Health and Learning Disabilities — CaPS-Q, CMU-Q

Students who have documented mental health conditions or learning disabilities may request academic accommodations.

Process:
1. Schedule a meeting with CaPS-Q
2. Provide documentation from a licensed professional
3. CaPS-Q reviews and coordinates accommodations with the Education Office

Accommodations may include:
- Extended test time
- Distraction-reduced testing environments
- Note-taking support
- Flexible attendance policies

Students are encouraged to register early in the semester.
