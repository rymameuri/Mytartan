Health Services & Policies — Health & Wellness, CMU-Q

The Director of Health and Wellness fosters student health and well-being through:

Health Education:
Shares resources and tools supporting informed decisions about physical, emotional, and social well-being. Runs evidence-based health promotion campaigns.

Wellness Coaching:
Assists students in identifying personal wellness goals and developing action plans. Supports healthy habits around sleep, nutrition, exercise, stress management, and self-care. Helps students identify barriers to well-being and develop strategies. Connects students to campus and community resources.

Health Promotion Initiatives:
Develops and implements campus-wide programs and events encouraging healthy behaviors. Coordinates health awareness campaigns focused on: Wellness Resources, Breast Cancer Awareness, Diabetes Awareness, Flu Campaigns, Women's and Men's Health, Nutrition, Stress Management, and more.

Health Forms and Immunizations:
The Health & Wellness office requires students to submit health forms and immunization records prior to or at the start of enrollment. Required immunizations include standard vaccinations required for Qatar and per CMU policy. Forms are submitted via Health Connect. Detailed instructions are provided to incoming students.
