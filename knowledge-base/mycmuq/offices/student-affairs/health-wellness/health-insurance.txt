Health Insurance — Health & Wellness, CMU-Q

All full-time students are required to carry health insurance meeting certain standards of coverage.

In the absence of adequate private health insurance, students are automatically enrolled in the university-provided student health insurance plan. The plan covers medical, dental, and vision.

Students may waive the university plan if they have comparable private insurance — the waiver form must be submitted with documentation by the published deadline.

Documents must be submitted through Health Connect (Messages tab); email submissions are not accepted for privacy reasons.

Failure to submit all required documents by deadline results in automatic enrollment and charges to the student account.
