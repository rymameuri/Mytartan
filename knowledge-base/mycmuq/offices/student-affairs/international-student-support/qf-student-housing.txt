QF Student Housing — International Student Support, CMU-Q

The Qatar Foundation residence halls create a learning community and supportive living environment where students engage in global leadership, sustainability, and diversity.

Rooms are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

Term Dates & Rates:
- Fall 2025: Aug 16 – Dec 19, 2025
- Winter 2025: Dec 20, 2025 – Jan 1, 2026
- Spring 2026: Jan 2 – May 14, 2026
- Summer 2026: May 15 – July 30, 2026
- Fall & Spring charged per semester; Winter & Summer charged per week (week = Friday 00:00 to Thursday 23:59)
- Housing reservations are made per semester (not annually)

Housing Deposit: 2,000 QAR upfront damage deposit required regardless of financial aid; refunded at end of stay.

Housing Fees:
Residence Hall Room: QAR 10,100 per semester / QAR 646 per week

Includes: Furnished room, utilities, internet, laundry facilities, common areas.

A Recommended Packing List and Frequently Asked Questions are also linked on portal.

QF Housing Portal: https://qf.starrezhousing.com
