Financial Wellness at CMU — International Student Support, CMU-Q

Personal financial well-being involves more than balancing a checkbook — it includes long-term vision, planning, and discipline. CMU is committed to student financial well-being.

Resources available:
- Achieving Financial Wellness — general financial literacy guidance
- Budget Worksheet — downloadable budgeting tool
- Strategies to Save Money — practical tips

Helpful Links:

- Free annual credit report (pulls from all three credit bureaus; helps spot identity theft)
- Determine Your Financial Aid & Repayment Options
- Fixed or Variable Interest Rates: How Do You Choose? (PDF)
- Credit Reports and Scores (USA.gov)

Questions about financial wellness and presentation requests may be directed to the Director of Health and Wellness and the Coordinator for International Student Services.
