Washington Semester Program — Study Abroad Experiences, CMU-Q

The Washington Semester Program allows students to spend a semester in Washington D.C. engaging with government, policy, and non-profit organizations.

Student Affairs provides support for students undertaking this program from CMU-Q, complementing the Education Office's academic advising on the program.

For academic requirements and application details, see the Education Office Washington Semester Program page.
