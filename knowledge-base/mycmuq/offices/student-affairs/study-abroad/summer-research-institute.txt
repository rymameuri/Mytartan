Summer Research Institute — Study Abroad Experiences, CMU-Q

The Summer Research Institute (SRI) provides CMU-Q students with opportunities to engage in faculty-led research over the summer.

The program supports students in gaining hands-on research experience, developing professional skills, and building academic credentials.

Application details, eligibility requirements, and past program information are available on the portal.

SRI is administered in coordination with CMU Pittsburgh and CMU-Q faculty.
