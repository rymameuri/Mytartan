Studying in Pittsburgh — Study Abroad Experiences, CMU-Q

CMU-Q students may study for a semester or year at the main CMU Pittsburgh campus.

This page provides information on:
- Eligibility criteria
- The application process
- Credit transfer policies
- Housing options in Pittsburgh
- Adjusting to campus life

Students are encouraged to connect with the Education Office and Student Affairs for advising.
