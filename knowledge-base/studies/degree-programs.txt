CMU-Q Undergraduate Degree Programs (Majors)

Carnegie Mellon University in Qatar (CMU-Q) offers four undergraduate Bachelor of Science degree programs:

1. Biological Sciences (BS)
   Focuses on the molecular, cellular, and systems-level study of living organisms. Students gain a strong foundation in biology, chemistry, and research methods.

2. Business Administration (BS)
   Covers core business disciplines including accounting, finance, marketing, operations, and strategy. Students develop analytical and leadership skills for careers in business and management.

3. Computer Science (BS)
   One of CMU's flagship programs. Students study algorithms, software systems, theory of computation, and applied computing. Known globally for its rigor and career outcomes.

4. Information Systems (BS)
   Bridges technology and business. Students learn to design and manage information systems, with coursework spanning databases, programming, business processes, and data analytics.

Declaring or Changing Your Major

Students who wish to declare or change their major should contact their academic advisor and the relevant department. Forms for major changes are available through the Student Forms page on Scotty.

Student-Defined Majors (SDM)

Students may design their own major through the Student-Defined Major (SDM) program, approved by the Associate Vice Provost's Office. This option is for subject areas not currently covered by existing departments. To apply, complete the SDM form and email it to qatareducationform@andrew.cmu.edu.
