The Student College, or StuCo, provides CMUQ students with the opportunity to teach a course about the topic of their choice and share their knowledge with other students.

Each StuCo course carries 3 units of credit, is graded Pass/No Pass, and they typically are offered as half-semester minis.

Both instructors and students receive credit for their work (up to a maximum of 9 units).

Current Spring 2026 StuCo

98486 – The Business of Sports
This course examine how all sports clubs balance performance and profit through analytics, economics, and strategy. From Moneybag to the European Super League, students explore global cases, debate profit vs. passion, and design real-world strategies for sustainable success.
Course Timings: Tuesday and Wednesday 5:00-6:20 pm
Instructor: Maya Al-Taji & Cas Leadbetter
