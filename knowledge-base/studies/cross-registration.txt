Students studying within Education City have a unique opportunity to gain depth and breadth through cross-registration at other Education City universities. Students may take courses outside of their major at Texas A&M University at Qatar, Georgetown University in Qatar, Northwestern University in Qatar, Virginia Commonwealth University in Qatar, and Weill Cornell Medicine – Qatar.

Important Note: We will begin accepting Fall 2026 Cross-Registration Forms on July 1, 2026. The deadline for submission is August 1, 2026, after which we will begin processing all applications.

CMU-Q Students — How to Cross-Register

1. After you have selected the course you wish to take, download the Education City Cross Registration Form.
2. Fill out the form online – handwritten forms will not be accepted.
3. Print it, sign it, and collect signatures from Sheila Rian and your advisor.
4. Email the completed cross registration form with a copy of your passport to the host school's registrar's email.

Basic cross-registration guidelines for CMU-Q students:
- Cross-registration is open to full-time students.
- Students may cross-register for one course per semester.
- When cross-registered, students need to adhere to the host school's academic calendar (start date, drop/withdrawal deadlines, final exam, etc.). If you choose to drop the class, you must drop it on SIO as well as with the host school. The status/grade that the host school reports on its transcript will be posted to your CMU transcript after the end of the semester. The grade you receive for your cross-registered course factors in your QPA.

Hosted Students (Taking Courses at CMU-Q)

How to cross-register:
1. Review course offerings in the Schedule of Classes. Be sure to select "Doha" as the "Teaching Location".
2. After selecting the course, download the Education City Cross Registration Form.
3. Fill out the form online — handwritten forms will not be accepted.
4. Print it, sign it, and collect all signatures.
5. Email the completed cross registration application form with a copy of the passport to qatareducationform@andrew.cmu.edu.
6. If a course is open, registration can be completed in two to three business days. If a course is full, your name will be added to the waitlist, and you will be welcome to attend until a seat opens or the end of the add-period (the 10th day of class).

Basic cross-registration guidelines for hosted students:
- Cross-registration is generally open to full-time students at the sophomore level and beyond, although exceptions can be made.
- Students may cross-register for one course per semester or a maximum of 9 units for courses less than 9-units.
- When cross-registered, students need to adhere to CMU-Q's academic calendar (start date, drop/withdrawal deadlines, final exam, etc.). If you choose to drop the class, you must contact Sheila Rian as well as your home school.
- Mid-semester grades will not be reported to your home institution.
- The grade that CMU-Q reports on our transcript will be posted to your home transcript after the end of the semester.
