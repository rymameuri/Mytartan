Internal Transfer Application

CMU-Q students who wish to apply for internal transfer into another department/major must follow the steps below and complete the application prior to the application deadline. Applications may be submitted in both the fall and spring semesters for all students except those in their first year.

- First Year Students should submit their application form after final grades have been submitted for their first year.
- Sophomore students and above should submit their application no later than 2 weeks prior to registration week in order to be considered for the following semester.

Desired Program Requirements for Transfer:

Biological Sciences — Must have completed:
- Calculus sequence (21-120 or 21-111/112)
- English (76-100 and 101)
- Modern Bio (03-121) minimum grade of B
- Minimum QPA of 2.0 (regardless if freshman)

Business Administration:
- May immediately qualify with a cumulative QPA of 3.0.
- If QPA is lower than 3.0, allow a trial semester: Fall sophomore must be typical of a full time BA student with at least 4 of the 5 courses being 21-XXX, 70-XXX, or 73-XXX, and a minimum semester performance of 3.0 in the BA major courses expected.

Computer Science — Must have completed:
- To be automatically accepted into CS, students need to have successfully completed 21-127, 15-112, and 21-120 (or the equivalent), and earned at least a 3.6 QPA in this set of required courses, in addition to having a minimum cumulative QPA of 3.0.
- If students do not meet these requirements, are in good academic standing, and are still interested in transferring into CS, these students are encouraged to discuss their case with the CS Associate Area head for special consideration on a case by case basis.

Information Systems:
- Must have a 3.5 cumulative QPA and a 'B' or better in 15-112 for immediate transfer.
- If the student does not meet these requirements, is still interested in transferring into IS, and is in good academic standing, the student is encouraged to discuss their case with the IS Associate Area Head for special consideration on a case-by-case basis, prior to applying.
